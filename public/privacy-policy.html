Privacy Policy – EYEPEK App

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the EYEPEK Android application (“App”), published under the name EYEPEK, handles information. The App provides information about EYEPEK education, college-support and IT services, courses, and enquiry options.

Information you enter in the App

The current version of the App may let you type information such as your name, mobile number, email address, course or service of interest, state or university, and a message in the enquiry form. These details are used only to display and prepare the enquiry form on your device. In the current version, the form does not submit information to an EYEPEK server, and the App does not create a user account or maintain an online enquiry record. Do not enter sensitive personal information in the form.

Information collection and sharing

The current version of the App does not transmit the enquiry form fields to EYEPEK or to an analytics or advertising provider. If you independently contact EYEPEK by phone, email, WhatsApp, or through the EYEPEK website, the information you choose to share will be handled by the relevant communication service and by EYEPEK to respond to your request. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Device permissions and storage

The App does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or SMS to provide its current features. Text entered into the enquiry form is held temporarily while the form is open and is not saved by the App as an account or enquiry history. Your Android device and operating system may maintain standard app or diagnostic information.

Advertising and analytics

The current version of the App does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics/tracking SDKs.

Children’s privacy

The App is a general information and service-enquiry application and is not designed specifically for children under 13. Children should use it with the guidance of a parent or guardian.

Data security and retention

Because the current App does not send enquiry-form data to an EYEPEK server or create App accounts, EYEPEK does not retain those form entries on its servers. Information you separately send to EYEPEK by email, phone, WhatsApp, or the website may be retained as needed to respond to and manage your enquiry, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Please contact us to ask about information you have directly shared with us.

Your choices and requests

You can close the enquiry form without submitting it. For questions or requests about information you have directly shared with EYEPEK, contact us using the details below. The current App does not offer account creation, so there is no App account to delete.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when the App’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this same URL with an updated revision date. Please review it periodically.
