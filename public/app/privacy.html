Privacy Policy

EYEPEK · Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains how the EYEPEK app (the "App") handles your information. The App lets you browse EYEPEK's education, IT, home and business services and prepare a booking request.

Information you enter

When you prepare a booking request, you may type your name, phone number, city or address, a preferred date, and a short note about your requirement.

How this information is used

  • It is used only to create your booking request message on your screen.
  • The App does not send this information to any EYEPEK server. The App does not have a user account or a database of bookings.
  • Your request reaches EYEPEK only when you choose to send it, by tapping the WhatsApp or email button. It is then shared through the WhatsApp or email app on your device, under their own terms and privacy policies. EYEPEK uses it to contact you about the service you asked for.

Information stored on your device

To save you from typing again, the App may remember your name, phone number, address and chosen language in your device's local storage. This stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing the App's data or site data in your device settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads in the App.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools in the App.
  • We do not sell your information.

Links and contact buttons

The App contains links that open other apps or websites, such as the phone dialer, WhatsApp, your email app and eyepek.com. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Children

The App is intended for general use and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and post the new version at this address.

Contact us

For questions about this policy, contact EYEPEK at Info.eyepek@gmail.com or +91 70043 74250.
