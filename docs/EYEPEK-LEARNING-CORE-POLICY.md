# EYEPEK Learning — Core Operating Policy

**Policy owner:** EYEPEK Administration  
**Applies to:** Students, Tutors, Institutions and EYEPEK administrators

## 1. Tutor status (Admin-only)
The three operational Tutor Status labels are visible only to authorized EYEPEK Admin accounts:
- **Pending:** Registration has been submitted; Admin review is pending.
- **Approved:** EYEPEK Admin has approved the Tutor. Approval alone does not mean a Student has been allocated.
- **Assigned:** EYEPEK Admin has allocated at least one active Student or learning service to the Tutor.

Students and Tutors do not control or change these status labels. Tutor approval and active account status are prerequisites for allocation.

## 2. Student registration and Tutor discovery
- Student registration is free.
- Students may search approved Tutor profiles using Tutor ID, name or subject for discovery and profile lookup.
- Search is not a booking, selection or allocation action.
- Only EYEPEK Admin may assign, replace or end a Student–Tutor allocation.

## 3. Service charges and access
- EYEPEK Admin sets all Student service fees and paid assignment charges.
- Applicable fees and rules must be shown before a paid service begins.
- A paid live session or paid assignment must not become accessible until payment is verified by EYEPEK.
- A pending, failed or unverified payment is not a successful payment.
- Razorpay integration is a release dependency for accepting online payments. Until the gateway is integrated and verified, do not represent a payment as collected or grant access based on a simulated payment.

## 4. Tutor responsibilities and compensation
- Tutors sign in with their unique EYEPEK Tutor ID and password.
- Tutors must not choose or allocate Students, set Student fees, or collect money directly from Students.
- Tutor compensation rates are fixed by EYEPEK Admin.
- Session and assignment work must be verified under Admin rules before compensation is approved.
- Tutors cannot mark their own compensation as paid. EYEPEK Admin controls approval and payout records.

## 5. Admin authority and quality control
EYEPEK Admin controls Tutor onboarding, approval, suspension, Student allocation, service pricing, session duration, assignment rules, quality review, payment verification, Tutor compensation and payout approval.

## 6. Release safety
A feature is not considered production-ready solely because its interface exists. Database migrations, API authorization, payment enforcement, classroom access rules and end-to-end tests must be completed before the corresponding service is advertised as operational.
